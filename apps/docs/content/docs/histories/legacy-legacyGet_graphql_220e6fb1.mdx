## 2026-11-12 — Enforce shared 100,000 requests/month allowance across free account projects

  - After: Free accounts share an authoritative 100,000 requests/month allowance across all free projects, API keys, and console sessions across legacy and v1 gateways, resetting on the 1st of each calendar month at 00:00 UTC alongside existing per-project minute and expensive-operation limits.
